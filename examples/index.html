The frame is the evidence

Judge only what the photo clearly proves

A photo can be valid and useful without earning full points. Full-point scoring depends only on what the submitted, unzoomed frame clearly shows—not what exists nearby or was obvious to the photographer.

Whole doorShow the complete doorway, not a cropped edge or fragment.
Visible roadThe road must appear in the frame, not merely connect to the square nearby.
Clear signThe sign must be unobstructed and recognizable, not tiny or partial.
Natural viewUse the hider's point of view without zooming or artificially tightening the frame.
Quick test for full points: Could another player identify every claimed clue from this photo alone, without being told what is just outside the frame? If not, the photo may still be valid, but the missing or unclear item does not count toward full points.

A rule issue, not bad photography

Retake required

Zoom can make a photo appear clearer while removing the wider context. Because clue photos must use the hider's natural point of view, a zoomed submission needs to be retaken.
