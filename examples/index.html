The frame is the evidence

Judge only what the photo clearly proves

A door, road, or sign does not count just because it exists nearby. It must be clearly visible in the submitted, unzoomed photo. These examples show why a photo that looks attractive or detailed can still be a weak clue.

Whole doorShow the complete doorway, not a cropped edge or fragment.
Visible roadThe road must appear in the frame, not merely connect to the square nearby.
Clear signThe sign must be unobstructed and recognizable, not tiny or partial.
Natural viewUse the hider's point of view without zooming or artificially tightening the frame.
Quick test: Could another player identify each claimed clue from this photo alone, without being told what is just outside the frame? If not, take another photo.
